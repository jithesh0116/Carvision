Upload a photo of your car from the front, side, or three-quarter view. CarVision's deep learning Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) with transfer learning analyzes visual patterns to recognize make and model, saving time and reducing manual selection mistakes.

An optimized Extreme Gradient Boosting regression model trained on comprehensive market attributes—including brand, model, variant, manufacturing year, mileage, transmission, and fuel type—captures non-linear relationships to calculate an accurate resale estimate.
